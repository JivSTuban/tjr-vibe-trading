# Lesson 49 — Verification Questions (owner: Jiv)

1. **Reaction-candle definition:** At an OB or FVG, what exactly counts as the single "reaction" candle that suffices for confirmation (engulfing, close direction into the trend, wick rejection depth)? [ref 04:49]
2. **Equilibrium double-gate:** For a bare-equilibrium entry, confirm the two required conditions are (a) a candle CLOSE back above the EQ line, AND (b) a BOS over the prior high — and whether both must occur on the same timeframe. [ref 04:32]
3. **Daily authority scope:** Confirm daily-bias-is-authoritative applies to intraday only, and specify the switch-over to weekly for swing trades. [ref 09:56]
4. **Building-block hit tolerance + expiry:** How close must price come to a pre-marked OB/FVG to count as "hit," and for how many candles/sessions does a marked building block remain valid before it's discarded? [ref 13:24]
5. **Target/RR rule:** TP selection is described as discretionary ("4H candle bodies/highs," "not too specific on mocks"). Is there a real-trade RR target or TP-laddering rule to encode, or is target-setting left to per-trade structure? [ref 05:24]
6. **"In line with the daily trend":** Define daily-trend direction for the never-fade-the-daily rule (last daily BOS vs. current swing). [ref 10:11]
