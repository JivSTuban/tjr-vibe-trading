**Key nuance — equilibrium confirmation strictness** [04:20–05:15]. When price comes
into equilibrium and you get **one bullish candle that does NOT close back above the
equilibrium line**, that is technically bullish confirmation but **not enough for him**.
It *would* be enough if price were reacting off an **order block** or filling a **fair
value gap** — but at bare equilibrium alone, you should **wait for the equilibrium point
to get closed above again**, plus a break of structure over the prior high, before
entering. (This directly extends lesson 46's "sweep+BOS alone isn't enough.")

## Codex interpretation

- **Daily-as-primary intraday rule** (clarifies lesson 47's open question): when weekly and daily disagree, **use the daily bias** for intraday, because "we're doing intraday, not swing." Weekly disagreement just marks a retrace context. Encode daily as the authoritative bias for intraday entries; weekly informs targets/retrace zones only.
- **Never trade retraces except on the weekly** [10:03]: entries must be in the daily-trend direction; do not fade the daily to trade a 4H/1H retrace.
- **Full long entry template (S&P mock):** all TFs aligned bullish + liquidity sweep (post-news) + BOS up + retest into OB/EQ/FVG + reaction confirmation → enter, **stop under the liquidity sweep**, targets at left-side 4H candle bodies/highs. Achieved ~+1.6R.
- **Equilibrium-confirmation strictness (machine-critical):** at *bare* equilibrium, require (a) a candle **closing back above** the equilibrium line AND (b) a **BOS over the prior high**. At an OB or FVG, a single reaction candle suffices. Distinct confirmation thresholds by building-block type.
- **"Primed for execution" pattern:** HTF all aligned + a smaller-TF opposite BOS into a building block = the execution trigger (the retrace fill), not a reversal signal.
- **No-execution-point = no-trade:** even full alignment does not fire an entry unless price reaches a pre-marked building block and gives a reaction (GU example). This is a hard AND-gate.
