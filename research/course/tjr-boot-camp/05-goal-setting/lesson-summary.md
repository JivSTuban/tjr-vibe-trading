# Lesson 05 — Goal Setting

## What TJR teaches

Day 5 is a "discipline / psychology type of video" [00:11]. TJR frames why psychology gets equal billing with trading: mentors say psychology is the majority of trading, yet their courses only cover a "little section" [00:30-00:38]. He wants to hand you "The X Factor" — the trait that separates a demo winner who chokes on live from someone who can actually execute [01:46-02:13]. Today's whole deliverable is a **goal-setting method** he learned through a basketball program [01:23-01:39].

**The structure (the core rule set).**
1. Write down **3 long-term end goals** [02:53-03:05]. These are "10 years down the line" super-long-term goals you don't yet know how to reach [03:40-03:43, 04:59-05:03]. Examples he offers: manage six figures in a trading account, make six figures in profit a month, a seven-figure account, buy a $10M house [03:48-04:34]. Being a funded trader is only a **medium-term** goal, not long-term [03:32-03:38]. Not everything has to be trading — these are life goals [04:36-04:52] — but he **advises at least one be trading-related** [07:07-07:13].
2. For **each** long-term goal, write **10 short-term goals / steps** [05:42-05:50]. That is **30 short-term goals total** (10 × 3) [06:27-06:33]. Trading examples: "I follow my trading plan every single day" [06:47-06:52], "I use risk management every single day" [07:25-07:28], "I journal my trades / review what I did right and wrong every single day" [08:30-08:36]. Short-term goals can also be outcome checkpoints: "I am profitable for the week" (a great place to start) then "I am profitable for the month" [10:12-10:27, 13:39-13:45].
3. **Complete the goals / take the steps daily.** The metaphor: Google-Maps the path; climb the mountain — "there's no elevator to the top," take steps and steps getting **1% better every single day** [11:31-11:43, 08:41-08:47 (earlier 1% mention 07:21-07:23)].
4. **Read AND vocalize your goals out loud, at least 5 times, every single day**, as if you are already there [16:07-16:31, 19:22-19:26]. Phrase them as **"I am" or "I will"** — never "I want" [16:52-17:08, 20:37-20:44]. Saying it out loud "drills it into your subconscious" until you believe it [20:26-20:35]. His personal ritual: goals written at the back of the notebook, then **rewritten every morning** (all 3 long-term + all 10 short-term), read + written + vocalized [19:48-20:21].

**Why it works / behavioral payoff.** After reading disciplined-trader affirmations 5× before the open, it becomes "really really difficult to over-leverage, to over-trade" [21:04-21:11]. TJR says this is what got him "locked in" — blinders on, stopped folding to emotions/pressure [21:15-21:44]. Long-term goals then get "smashed / demolished" [11:03-11:09].

**Deadlines & his own example (context).** He puts a deadline on goals: his stated goal is **100,000 YouTube subscribers within the year** (they'd hit **30,000 followers that day**, TikTok + YouTube roughly even after his old TikTok got banned) [14:03-15:00]. "Goals are just goals until you take action" [24:29-24:35].

**Homework.** Buy a notebook (repurpose a school notebook into a trading journal) [22:59-23:35]; write 3 long-term end goals + 10 short-term goals each; complete them daily; read + vocalize goals daily [21:52-23:23]. Warning: if you're too embarrassed to say your goals out loud where others can hear, "you're not cut out for this... you do not want it enough" [23:25-24:05].

## Codex interpretation

- This is a **process-goal framework**, not a trade setup. It yields **behavioral guardrails** for the auto-trading operator/psychology layer, not chart signals. No instrument, timeframe, or entry rule is taught.
- Load-bearing numbers to encode as configuration/checklist: **3** long-term goals, **10** short-term goals each → **30** total; recite **≥5×/day**; **1% improvement/day** ethos; goal horizon **~10 years** (long-term) vs "funded trader" = medium-term.
- The strongest transferable guardrail: gate live sizing behind a **daily pre-market affirmation/checklist ritual** ("I follow my trading plan," "I use risk management," "I journal every day"). TJR's causal claim is that this ritual measurably suppresses over-leverage and over-trading [21:04-21:11] — testable as a compliance checklist, not a market rule.
- Suggested guardrails derived (see proposed-rules.yaml): consistency/process goals before profit-size goals; weekly-profitable before monthly-profitable before scaling; require at least one trading-related process goal.
- **NOT rules:** the $10M house, seven-figure account, Lambo, and 100k-subs-by-year-end are illustrative/personal goals — do not model. "1% better every day" is motivational framing, not a literal 1% metric.
