# Ambiguities — Lesson 05 (Goal Setting)

- **No market/trade rules exist in this lesson.** It is purely a psychology/goal-setting framework. No instrument, timeframe, direction, entry, SL/TP, or risk % is taught here (risk-management numbers are deferred to other lessons). Do not derive any chart signal from this video.
- **"1% better every single day"** [11:31, 07:21] is motivational framing, NOT a literal measurable 1% KPI. Confidence: medium; requires_confirmation:true. Do not encode as a numeric daily-return target.
- **Profit checkpoints have no numbers.** "Profitable for the week / for the month" [10:12] give no % or $ target, no minimum streak length, and no size-increase schedule. The consistency ladder (weekly -> monthly -> scale) is directional only.
- **3 and 10 counts:** he says "make sure it's 10 for each" and "three long-term end goals" — treat as target counts, but whether they are strict maxima/minima is not fully explicit. Non-trading (life) goals count toward the 3.
- **"At least one trading-related goal"** [07:07] is advice ("I advise"), not an absolute mandate — encode as a soft warning.
- **">=5x vocalization -> less over-trading"** [21:04-21:11]: TJR's causal claim is anecdotal ("once I started doing this I got locked in"), not measured. Encode the ritual as a checklist; do NOT assume a proven effect size.
- **Personal/illustrative goals are NOT rules:** $10M house, seven-figure account, Lambo [04:25-04:34, 05:01], and "100,000 subscribers within this year" / "30,000 followers today" [14:03-15:00] are examples of HIS goals, not trading targets or datapoints to model.
- **Deadline guidance is vague:** "put a deadline on it" [15:13] gives no method for choosing a realistic deadline length.
- ASR note: "jizz"/"jits" = TJR's verbal greeting/signature, not a term. No SMC jargon (OB/BOS/MSS/FVG/liquidity) appears in this lesson, so no jargon corrections were needed.
