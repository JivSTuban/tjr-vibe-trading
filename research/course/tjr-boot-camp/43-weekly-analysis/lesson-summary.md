## What TJR teaches

This is a weekend/pre-week routine done live with the Discord: a top-down weekly
markup of the coming week's news calendar plus a per-instrument bias.

**News-calendar review first.** TJR walks the week [00:31]:

- These **news events happen an hour before market opens** [01:49].

- Process point: even on no-trade (CPI/PPI) days he still wants to **get on Zoom and mark out levels** and show how the market reacts off them, rather than saying "CPI day, no trades, no Zoom" [01:29–01:45].

**Per-instrument top-down bias.** He treats weekly analysis exactly like any market-open markup: mark building blocks, note likely retrace areas, note draws on liquidity, do top-down analysis [08:34–08:47].

Now **bullish weekly, bullish daily**. A **daily order block** price will likely draw back into once more; price tested equilibrium (daily EQ ≈ weekly EQ) [04:03–04:10]. On the daily he'd like a retrace after the NFP boom into an **order block caused by NFP** (swept liquidity, rallied) [05:24–05:35].

Rule stated: **follow the weekly trend until the monthly proves it wrong** — you won't know the monthly wants higher until a **weekly break of structure**, and there hasn't even been a daily one, so continue with the **bearish bias** [08:07–08:24].

**Method + mindset close** [08:26–09:16]: "that's how I do weekly analysis. I treat it as I do any other pair, as if it were market open, by marking out building blocks … this is probably where price will retrace to, these are the draws on liquidity, and we just do top-down analysis." Give yourself an overview of where price could draw; if you get proved wrong, "you change your bias, you adapt to it."

## Codex interpretation

- Weekly analysis = a scheduled **top-down markup ritual** (monthly → weekly → daily → 4H → 1H) producing (a) a per-instrument directional bias and (b) an ordered list of draw-on-liquidity / building-block targets. Machine value: pre-compute HTF bias + target ladder per instrument once per week; intraday setups must agree with it.
- **News gate**: flag CPI/PPI (and unemployment claims, GBP releases) days as no-trade or observe-only. The stated timing — news "an hour before market opens" and GBP news "around 11 AM US time during London" — should become a calendar-driven trading-window blocker (needs exact timezone confirmation).
- **Structure hierarchy rule**: continue with the current-HTF-trend bias until a *break of structure on that same HTF* flips it; a lower-TF break in the opposite direction is treated as a retrace of the higher TF (recurs across lessons 47 & 49).
- Bias is **revisable**: "get proved wrong → change bias → adapt" — encode bias as a mutable state that HTF structure events can flip, not a static per-week constant.
