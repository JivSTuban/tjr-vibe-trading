# Lesson 12 — Liquidity Pt 3 (Full overview + how to use it)

## What TJR teaches

Part 3 (final) of the liquidity thread. Previews the upcoming curriculum order: after liquidity → **fair value gap series → order block series → equilibrium series** ("the building blocks we need to actually play trades") [00:19-00:57]. Repeated caveat: liquidity is NOT a standalone strategy — "it's a piece that we can plug in to a strategy... not end-all be-all" [01:45-01:52].

**Recap.** Why use liquidity: it's where market makers fill orders (many people entering/exiting → lots of money transacted). When liquidity ISN'T being swept/induced, price is "chippy choppy / tinky tacky" and they can't fully change direction [03:06-03:29].

**Marking liquidity: prominent highs and lows.** We label highs and lows as liquidity, "most importantly prominent highs and lows" [03:35-04:01]. What counts as prominent "varies from timeframe to timeframe" — no formula given; he marks them visually [04:03-04:13]. Examples of prominence: start of a big trend, double tops, a high that broke structure on the daily [04:39-05:44].

**Liquidity as a magnet.** "We can think of liquidity essentially as a magnet for price — that is where price is drawing to, because that's where the market makers want to push price so they can get their orders filled" [05:52-06:09]. Any form of liquidity = a magnet, NOT a buy/sell signal on touch. "It doesn't mean when this high gets hit we press sell... if you did that you would lose way more than you win. We wait for confirmation through break of structure / other confluences" [06:24-06:52].

**The confirmed sequence (entries).** Wait for the liquidity level to get hit → then watch price reaction. No reaction = no trade. Reaction (a break of structure) = act / take a trade [06:52-07:03, 07:22-07:38]. For shorts: take out a high → BOS to the downside → any other confluence → go short [09:52-10:09].

**Liquidity for take-profit targets.** Liquidity is also the exit magnet. If bearish bias, target previous prominent LOWS (that's where market makers exit shorts — they also need entering/exiting counterparties to close size without slippage) [07:42-09:29, 10:10-10:22]. Mirror for longs → target previous prominent highs.

**Worked chain (repeated across examples):** liquidity sweep → break of structure → (optionally) price fills a fair value gap / imbalance or reacts off a hidden order block → enter → target the previous prominent opposite-side liquidity. Timeframe scaling shown: e.g. daily bias, enter off a 4h FVG, or scale down to 5m for the entry.

**Chart examples cited:**
- S&P 500 (daily): prominent-high labeling walkthrough; sweep → BOS down → target previous prominent lows [02:45-06:09, 10:29-12:57].
- GBPUSD (4h→1h): 4h highs not hit on 4h but swept on 1h → BOS with a specific candle → retrace up into an order block → capitulate down, taking prominent low + filling imbalances [13:03-14:11].
- Second GBPUSD sequence: highs barely wicked → BOS at a low (down candle/up candle) → scale to 5m, imbalance fills → confirmation → go short toward lows/imbalance [14:11-14:51].
- Gold: lows swept → quick regain / closure above → dip into imbalance (entry zone) → price draws to previous highs → orders liquidated/filled [15:08-15:39].
- Many rapid flip-through 1m/15m "sweep rally / sweep drop" confirmations [15:45-17:19].

## Codex interpretation

- **Liquidity = bidirectional magnet.** Use marked prominent highs/lows both as the DRAW (where price is heading) and as TP targets (opposite-side previous liquidity). Codable: for a short, TP = nearest untapped previous prominent low; for a long, TP = nearest untapped previous prominent high.
- **Full entry model now explicit (still multi-signal):** sweep of prominent liquidity → BOS in the reversal direction → confluence (FVG fill and/or order block reaction, ideally on a lower timeframe) → enter. Never fade the level on touch. This supersedes/expands Pt1 (mechanism) + Pt2 (location).
- **Multi-timeframe entry pattern:** identify liquidity/BOS on a higher TF, then scale down (e.g., 4h→1h→5m) to refine the entry on an FVG/imbalance fill. Concrete TF combos are illustrative, not fixed.
- **"Prominent" remains undefined** — the single biggest automation gap. Needs an operational swing-prominence definition (see ambiguities).
- FVG/imbalance and order block are used here but their full definitions come in later dedicated series — treat as forward references.
