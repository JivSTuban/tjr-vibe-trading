# Ambiguities — Lesson 12 (Liquidity Pt 3)

- **"Prominent" high/low is undefined** — this is the single biggest automation blocker. TJR marks prominence visually and says it "varies from timeframe to timeframe." Cited signals of prominence (not a formula): start of a big trend, double top, a high/low that broke structure on the relevant timeframe. Needs an operational definition (e.g., pivot with N bars each side, or swing that itself produced a BOS).
- **Confluence count**: sequence is sweep -> BOS -> confluence, but whether ONE confluence (FVG or OB) suffices or MULTIPLE are required is not stated. Examples use varying combinations.
- **Take-profit selection**: "target previous prominent lows/highs" — but multiple candidates exist; "nearest untapped" vs "next major pool" not specified.
- **Timeframe pairing**: HTF->LTF scaling shown (daily/4h -> 1h/5m) but not fixed to a spec; illustrative only.
- **FVG / imbalance / order block** used operationally but full definitions are forward references (upcoming FVG series starts lesson 14, order block + equilibrium series after).
- **Sweep penetration threshold** still undefined (carried over from Pt 2): "barely wicked" examples imply shallow overshoot qualifies.
- ASR jargon confirmed: "GBP USD"=GBPUSD; gold=XAUUSD; "imbalance"/"fair value gap"/"liquidity void" treated as synonyms; "hidden order block" = an OB inside the retrace zone.
