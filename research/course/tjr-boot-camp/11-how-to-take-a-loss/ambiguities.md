# Ambiguities — Lesson 11 (How to Take a Loss)

- **Cooldown after a loss**: TJR says no revenge/over-trading but gives no numeric cooldown or trade cap here. Lesson 13 caps daily risk (1–3%/day), which indirectly limits how many losses can compound in a day.
- **Demo-to-live gate**: "get profitable on demo" — no defined threshold (sample size, win rate, % return, duration).
- **Degraded-state filters**: fatigue and emotional state are not machine-detectable — require a manual kill switch. News-day avoidance is deferred to the news lesson (day 19).
- **Dollar anecdotes** ($9k GU loss, $10k loss, 112k flip) are personal/motivational, NOT parameters.
- **"one-month turnaround"** to profitability post-demo is a rough personal estimate, not a target.
