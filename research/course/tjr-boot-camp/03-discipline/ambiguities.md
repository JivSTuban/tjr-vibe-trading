# Lesson 03 — Ambiguities

## "No bailing" / "confident enough to take trades" need a setup definition
TJR says the profitability gap is bailing out of valid trades and not being confident enough to take them [03:34-03:57]. Both require an objective "valid setup" definition (and legitimate early-exit/invalidation conditions) that this lesson does not provide — those live in the strategy lessons. Until then, r01/r02 cannot distinguish a fear-exit from a rule-based exit, or a hesitation-skip from a correct skip.

## 21-day / 60-day habit numbers are hedged heuristics
"A statistic that says habits form over — I believe it's 21 days... a routine/ritual over 60 days" [09:40-10:15]. TJR half-hedges ("I believe"). These are pop-psychology figures, not validated. Encoded as target milestones only (r04), confidence medium, requires_confirmation.

## "1% better every single day" is not measurable
[26:14-26:24] Aspirational KPI. For arbitrary user-chosen habits there is no objective way to quantify a daily 1% delta. Kept as north-star framing, not an enforceable metric.

## Emotion rules conflict across lessons
This lesson: "the motivation is gone and the discipline is all you have left" → execute the plan regardless of feelings [27:07-27:18]. Lesson 11 (how to take a loss): take days off when emotionally compromised. Reconciliation proposed in r05 (follow the plan while trading; a manual kill switch may disable a whole session), but the boundary is not stated by TJR and should be confirmed.

## Trading guardrails stated without numbers
"No over-trading, no over-leveraging, risk management, no emotion" [24:56-25:10]. No concrete max-trades/day, leverage cap, or per-trade risk % given here. r06 is a placeholder; real values must come from the risk-management / loss lessons.

## Habit is user-chosen and non-trading
The Day-3 homework habit (gym, reading, walking) is deliberately personal and not a trading action. So r03 is a human accountability/streak tracker, not an auto-trading rule. The "done" criteria for the chosen habit are user-defined.

## Check-in cadence is social, not systemic
"I'll check in every other discipline video" [26:53-27:31] is a YouTube-audience accountability mechanic, not a system parameter. Noted for completeness only.
