## What TJR teaches

### Why this lesson exists
The video was prompted by a CPI day. TJR was on a Discord Zoom call and "raged" at how many people traded during, before, and after CPI [01:16–01:33]. The lesson is framed as covering "everything about news" so it never has to be re-covered [01:39–01:54].

### Where he gets his news — the calendar tool
- He uses **Forex Factory** — `forexfactory.com` [01:59–02:05].
- Raw calendar "is a lot to take in," so he **filters it** [02:20–02:26].
- **Folder color coding** [02:26–02:41]:
  - **Yellow folders** = "random stupid news events that happen day in and day out that don't really affect the market" → he removes/ignores them [02:26–02:38]. (low impact)
  - **Gray folders** = bank holidays / non-trading days → he keeps them for awareness [02:38–02:43].
  - **Orange folders** = medium impact (grouped with red in his "always wait for news" rule) [06:10–06:16].
  - **Red folders** = high impact — the ones that cause "havoc in the market" [03:53–04:06].
- He **filters out currencies he does not trade** [02:43–03:31]. He does NOT trade: AUD, CAD, CHF, CNY/Chinese, EUR, NZD pairs. He DOES trade: **GBP/USD, GBP/JPY, gold, and the S&P 500** [03:05–03:31]. He notes **S&P 500 news events are covered by USD** [03:28–03:31].
- After deselecting, he clicks **"apply filter"** and the calendar collapses to only relevant rows; the tool **labels which currency each news event is for** [03:33–03:45].
- Forex Factory **auto-detects your timezone** and displays release times in your local time [04:11–04:20].

### The always-avoid releases (write these down)
TJR says: "there's a couple news events that I always avoid you guys should write these down" [06:57–07:09]:
1. **CPI** [07:01–07:11]
2. **PPI** [07:01–07:16]
3. **FOMC** [07:01–07:20]
4. **NFP — non-farm payroll** ("anything that says non-farm... non-farm employment... non-farm payroll — nope, no go") [07:22–07:29]

"Those four I do not trade whatsoever" [07:29–07:32]. On a day with any of these four he simply does not trade: "just call it quits bro, sleep in, live to trade another day" [23:02–23:05].

### The core avoidance rule
- **If there is a red news folder, no matter what, he ALWAYS waits until the news comes out** [03:56–04:06].
- CPI "always affects the market" so on CPI he stays out entirely [06:02–06:04].
- **General rule for red AND orange folders: always wait for news to come out, let price develop, see if it drastically affected the market (large wicks, large moves). If it does — do NOT trade. If it doesn't, you can potentially look for a trade but stay defensive, reduce risk** [06:10–06:57].
- He shows CPI-day charts (GBP/JPY, gold) with "massive wicks" right when CPI dropped — "not price action that you are able to trade, it is unprobable price action" [04:39–05:08].

### Stated time buffers (AFTER the release)
- If news "doesn't affect the market for like after 15 or 20 minutes after it comes out — so at like 8:45 or like at 8:50" he would have been okay to trade [05:46–05:59]. (Implies an ~8:30 release time.)
- Repeated as a general rule: after a non-forbidden release, **wait "15, 20, 30 minutes, let price develop, then you can look to take a trade"** [10:17–10:27].
- Friday example: market opens **9:30**, news at **10:00** → he will "not trade until 10:00 hits, and still won't trade until like 10:30 hits because we need price to develop" (i.e., waits ~30 min past the release) [08:47–09:14].
- He will check the market **"after 8:30"** on FOMC/heavy days and decide based on how price develops [08:22–08:27].

### Multi-red-folder days
A day "flooded with news" / "three red folders" is a "terrible probability day to trade" [04:23–04:36]. Even if the releases aren't one of the forbidden four, many red folders = low probability → de-risk, and if the market makes a big move, "we're done" [08:36–08:44]. When all red folders are USD, he'd only consider his non-USD pair (GBP/JPY) [08:03–08:09].

### How to read the actual news numbers
- Columns: **Forecast** = what analysts/Forex Factory predict; **Actual** = the number that comes out; **Previous** also shown [10:34–11:01]. Before release the Actual cell is **blank** [11:48–12:04].
- Click the little folder to expand details (source e.g. Bureau of Labor Statistics, "Measures", and the "usual effect") [11:09–11:20].
- The **"usual effect"** line tells the news bias: **"if Actual is greater than Forecast, then it is good for the currency"** [12:07–12:27]. Conversely **less than forecast = bad for the currency** [14:22–14:38].
- **"Good for the currency" = good for the USD (the DXY), NOT the S&P 500** [12:44–13:09]. When DXY goes up, anything against the dollar goes down [13:14–13:17].
- So **good for USD → bad for SPX, bad for gold, bad for GU (GBP/USD), bad for EU, bad for AUD/USD, bad for any XXX/USD (incl. crypto BTC/USD)** [13:37–14:15].

### Pair mechanics — which side of the slash
- A pair is a **fraction**: numerator on top, denominator on bottom [19:35–19:47].
- **Good news for the currency on TOP → pair goes up. Bad news for TOP → down. Good news for the BOTTOM currency → pair goes DOWN (inverse). Bad news for BOTTOM → up** [19:50–20:10].
- Example: GBP/USD — good GBP news → up; good USD news → down [16:37–19:12]. USD/JPY — USD on top, so good USD news → up [19:16–19:32].
- "Whatever's on top it's positive correlation, whatever's on bottom it's inversed" [22:00–22:14].

### News bias is NOT the whole story
News bias just "helps us understand news data" — it is **not the be-all** [22:37–22:44]. He shows a case where CPI came out **less than forecast (bias = bad for USD) yet DXY rallied / S&P fell** — "price action is superior, that's why we have to wait for news to come out" [15:07–16:30]. So the bias can be faded by actual price action.

### Process discipline
- Check Forex Factory **every single morning before trading** [24:11–24:17].
- "Look for a reason NOT to trade, not a reason to trade" [09:45–09:52].
- Don't get caught in a trade then have news drop 5 min later and stop you out [24:17–24:23].
- If it's CPI/PPI/FOMC/NFP: don't trade at all [23:02–23:05].

## Codex interpretation (inference toward machine rules — NOT TJR's exact words)

- **News-avoidance module inputs:** an economic calendar feed (Forex Factory-equivalent) with per-event: currency, impact tier (red/high, orange/medium, yellow/low, gray/holiday), scheduled datetime (localized), forecast, actual, previous, and "usual effect" direction.
- **Hard block set:** event name matches {CPI, PPI, FOMC / interest-rate / Fed, NFP / non-farm payroll} on any traded pair's currency → block ALL new entries for the whole session/day. This is the strongest, most concrete rule.
- **Impact-tier block:** any red-folder (high-impact) event on a currency in the traded pair → block new entries from `T_before` minutes before until `T_after` minutes after the release; extend the block if price shows displacement/large wicks post-release. TJR gives `T_after` ≈ 15–30 min (use 30 as conservative default). He does NOT give an explicit `T_before` number → must be confirmed (see ambiguities).
- **Directional bias helper (advisory, not entry trigger):** `bias(currency) = good if actual > forecast else bad`. Map to pair: currency is numerator → same direction; denominator → inverse. USD "good" → USD-quoted risk assets (SPX, gold, XXX/USD) bearish. Flag as advisory only — TJR explicitly says price action overrides bias.
- **Risk reduction on heavy-news days:** if ≥2 red folders on traded currencies, cut risk / prefer no-trade even outside the block window.
