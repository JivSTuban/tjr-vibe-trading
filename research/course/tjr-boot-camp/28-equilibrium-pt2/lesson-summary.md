**Equilibrium recap** `[01:01]`, `[18:20]`:
- "It's a **retracement tool** that allows us to measure a **premium** and a **discount** market for smart money to enter." **Measure from the swing high to the swing low (or low to high) and find the 50% mark.** Restated at the end: whenever price makes a **new high in an uptrend**, drag the fib **from the low up to the high**; whenever a **new low is made in a downtrend**, drag **from the high down to the low.** Re-draw as new swings form.

**Premium / Discount — corrected and precise** `[01:39]`–`[02:24]`, `[02:54]`–`[03:07]`:
- **Premium = above the 50% mark = expensive = SELL/short zone.** "Everything from the 50 mark up there is a premium."
- **Discount = 0.5 down to 0 = cheap / on-sale = BUY/long zone.** "The discount [is] from that 0.5 down to the zero… everything within here is a discount because it's below the 50 mark."
- **The smart-money rule (stated explicitly):** "**Smart money never buys in a premium, [never] shorts in a premium; they always buy and [short] in a discount.**" (This corrects/clarifies the Day 26 verbal slip. Canonical: banks BUY at a DISCOUNT / SELL at a PREMIUM. See ambiguities for the minor wording tangle.)
- In a **downtrend**, once a new low forms, the zone from that low **up to** the swing high above 50% is a **premium** — smart money is NOT shorting there; they wait for price to get "on sale" (a discount) to short. (Note: in a downtrend "discount"/"premium" are still defined off the same 50% of the high→low measurement.)

## Codex interpretation (labeled inference toward machine rules)

- **Equilibrium = 0.5 fib of the reference swing.** Reference swing = the most recent impulse swing in the direction of bias: in an uptrend, low→high of the latest higher-high leg; in a downtrend, high→low of the latest lower-low leg. **Re-anchor on each new swing extreme.** (Day 28 finally pins down the reference-swing question left open in Day 26: it's the current-trend swing, re-drawn as new highs/lows print.)
- **Zone gate (corrected, canonical):** `price above 0.5 → PREMIUM → short-only`; `price at/below 0.5 → DISCOUNT → long-only`. Banks buy discount / sell premium.
- **Fib/Gann settings:** display 0, 0.5, 1. The **0.5–0.618 band** is the equilibrium/OTE region worth watching (TJR ties the popular 50%/61.8% fib retrace to equilibrium).
- **Entry = equilibrium (zone filter) + a trigger:** price retraces to equilibrium AND an **FVG or OB sits at equilibrium** AND a **confirming BOS/MSS** on a lower TF → enter; **stop beyond the swing extreme**; **TP = successive prior liquidity pools (scale out / multiple TPs).** Highest conviction: FVG/imbalance located exactly at equilibrium.
- **Multi-TF stack:** measure equilibrium on HTF (monthly/daily) → await tap → refine entry on LTF (4h→1h→15m) with OB/FVG + BOS. Align with HTF/weekly bias (short in downtrend, long in uptrend).
- Reconciles the Order-Blocks-pt3 deferred "OB off the first retracement in equilibrium" — the OB/FVG must sit in the discount (long) / premium (short) at the 0.5 level to qualify.
