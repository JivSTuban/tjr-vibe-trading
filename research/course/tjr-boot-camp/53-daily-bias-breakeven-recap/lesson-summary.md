# Boot Camp Day 52: Daily Bias + Break Even Trading Recap — Lesson Summary

Primary topic: example (live daily bias + a low-conviction GU trade closed at ~break-even)

## What TJR teaches

**News:** only Empire Manufacturing Index (already out); won't alter bias, but it's noted [00:02].

### Daily bias, pair by pair
- **SPX:** weekly + daily bullish; next draw on liquidity = monthly highs. Daily uptrend but **4H downtrend** → wants price to retrace into imbalances / daily equilibrium; "not necessarily shorts we want to take." Bearish 4H, bullish daily, 1H unclear → "S&P doesn't look great today; ideally sit this one out and wait for price to draw lower for new longs" [00:12].
- **Gold:** weekly downtrend, daily uptrend → look for buys off the daily trend; but 4H broke structure down and 1H is bearish → "don't necessarily want to be looking for a trade." Steering clear [02:16].
- **GU (GBPUSD):** weekly + daily uptrend, 4H + 1H breaking down → "you don't want to be trading down when you have all those high timeframes saying up." A mix of biases; some [\h] you don't want to take [04:24].
- **GJ (GBPJPY):** weekly bullish, daily bearish, but broke structure and tapped into a **daily order block**; got an **hourly BOS down**. Marks London session high (draw), untouched liquidity, a big FVG/liquidity void below. "This looks like the best trade… I'm going to camp out on GJ today." Wanted a short off the setup [06:13].

### The trade he actually took — GU (the wrong one)
- He took **GU instead of the GJ he'd flagged as best** [11:38]. Rationale: daily was bullish but "real overextended," at daily equilibrium; not a great trade, just a *potential* trade [12:31]. **Severely de-risked — ~15 lots vs his usual ~100 lots (about 1/10 size)**; stop a bit larger than usual for GU [13:02].
- Entry: saw price sweep highs (called hourly liquidity), with 4H BOS down + huge equilibrium below + bearish 4H/1H → "enough bearish bias to potentially take a trade." Entered off **highs swept → break of structure down**; stop above Asian session highs = **15-pip stop** [13:24].
- **Outcome:** got stuck consolidating in profit for ~2–2.5 hours; when price broke structure up he exited. **Closed for +$199.09 — essentially break-even** [17:37][18:15].
- Self-critique: "**Don't take this trade.** …this isn't even a trade I'd have wanted to take in the first place," glad it ended in profit rather than a loss [15:41]. It was too late in the day (2pm local) and low-conviction.

### The A+ he skipped — GJ
- The GJ setup was the best available: daily bearish, tapped the daily order block, then price drew to the **Asian session high** (past the London high) and reacted down with a **break of structure** [16:24]. "This is probably the best trade we could have taken today" [16:43]. He didn't take it because it pushed past his level before reacting.

### Rule reinforced
- "No reason to be going short on a pair when the daily is in bullish conditions and we start to see bullish confirmation on lower timeframes — that's never a good thing to do" [17:16]. Closing at break-even was fine: he stuck to his bias/plan and avoided risk late in the day [17:46].

## Codex interpretation (explicitly inferred — NOT verbatim rules)

- **A+ vs skip contrast:** the *skip-worthy* trade (GU) was counter to full HTF alignment / an overextended-at-equilibrium "potential" trade; the *A+* (GJ) had daily-order-block mitigation + draw to a named session-liquidity high + reaction + BOS in the daily-bias direction. Scorer should reward HTF-order-block + named-liquidity-draw + reaction + trend-aligned BOS, and penalize "overextended at equilibrium, mixed-timeframe" setups.
- **De-risking a low-conviction trade:** TJR sized a marginal trade at ~1/10 normal (15 vs 100 lots). Supports a *conviction-scaled position size* — but taking the trade at all contradicted his own rule; a machine should prefer *no trade* over a de-risked marginal trade.
- **Consolidation-time exit:** he exited after ~2–2.5h of chop when structure broke against him — supports a time-in-trade / range-stall exit condition.
- Numeric anchors: **15-pip stop** on GU; usual size ~100 lots, de-risked to **15 lots**; result **+$199.09** (break-even). Instrument: GBPUSD (taken), GBPJPY (skipped A+).
