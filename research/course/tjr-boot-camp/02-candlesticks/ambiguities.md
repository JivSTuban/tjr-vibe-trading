# Ambiguities — Lesson 02 (Candlesticks)

- **No numeric thresholds for "strong" vs "weak" close.** TJR uses "big body," "fat ass wick," "long wick," "small body," "tiny wick" entirely qualitatively [09:50, 15:14, 17:49]. There is NO body:wick ratio, tick/pip, or percentage that defines a strong close, a long wick, or a doji body size. Any machine rule must have this threshold supplied by Jiv.
- **Doji geometry undefined.** He shows a "candlestick patterns cheat sheet" and names doji, long-legged doji, cross doji, inverse cross doji, dragonfly doji, and hammer [09:01-09:16] but never gives the geometric definition of any of them. He also asserts "long-legged doji's and doji's are the same thing" [09:04], which conflicts with standard charting (long-legged doji has notably longer wicks). Confirm intended definitions.
- **"More than two candles" vs single-candle list.** He says he hates every pattern with "more than two candles" [08:03] but the only patterns he keeps are single-candle forms [08:17]. Unclear whether any 2-candle pattern (e.g., engulfing) is acceptable.
- **Sweep / liquidity-grab depth undefined.** He says a long wick "more than likely was taking this liquidity" [10:35] and describes a "confirmation candle" that "sweeps liquidity" [16:10], but no definition of how far past a level = a sweep. (Liquidity itself is a later thread — lesson 08.)
- **Timeframe of decisions not fixed.** Demonstrated on SPX 5-minute, Gold daily, and Gold weekly-ish candles interchangeably. Which timeframe governs bias vs entry is not specified here.
- **Instrument tickers used in homework:** SPX = S&P 500; "GU" / "g-u" = GBP/USD; "GJ" = GBP/JPY [19:02-19:06]. ASR renders these as loose letters.
- **ASR / jargon notes:**
  - "jizz"/"jits"/"jets" = TJR's verbal greeting/signature, NOT a term.
  - "bowls" [10:11] = ASR corruption of "bulls."
  - "the clothes" [05:23] and "bullish clothes" [19:44] = ASR corruption of "the close" / "bullish close."
  - "Buzz" [03:52] = filler/ASR artifact, not a term.
  - "Wix don't lie" [20:47] = the saying "Wicks don't lie"; TJR is unsure of the source and whether it's actually "fibs don't lie."
  - "highway to the upside" [15:31] = ASR corruption of "high wick to the upside."
