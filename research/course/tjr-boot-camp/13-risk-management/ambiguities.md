# Ambiguities — Lesson 13 (Risk Management)

- **% base**: "1-3% of your account" — of starting equity, current equity, or high-water mark? Not stated. Assume current equity; confirm.
- **Daily cap accounting**: Does a WINNING trade free up daily risk budget for another trade? Does a realized LOSS count against the cap, or only risk-allocated-at-entry? TJR only sums risk-at-entry in his examples. Assume allocated-risk-at-entry, no top-up on wins; confirm.
- **"Go heavier when biases align"**: no quantified mapping from conviction/confluence count to the 1%->3% dial. Needs a defined conviction score.
- **Max trades/day**: no explicit number; only the implicit bound from the 3%/day cap (3 trades at 1%). An explicit hard cap would need confirmation.
- **Position-size formula**: deferred by TJR to an external calculator. Per-instrument value tables (pip value, futures point/tick value, option premium behavior) and rounding policy must be supplied externally.
- **Futures stop-loss examples** (2-point, 5-point, 10-point) are illustrative of how stop distance varies the size — not prescribed stop distances.
- **NOT parameters (anecdotal)**: $60K/day upside, $27K single-trade loss (~2 weeks prior), $100K/year, "2 of 10 win," "lose 80%," "112K flip" (from lesson 11). The "80% loss at 1% => 20% account remaining" is usable only as a worst-case sanity check.
