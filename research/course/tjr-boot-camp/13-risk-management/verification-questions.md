# Verification Questions — Lesson 13 (owner: Jiv)

1. Risk % base: starting equity, current equity, or high-water mark? (Affects compounding/de-risking behavior.)
2. Daily cap mechanics: does a winning trade free up more daily risk budget? Does a realized loss consume the cap or only risk-at-entry?
3. Should there be an explicit hard max-trades-per-day, or rely solely on the 3%/day aggregate cap?
4. Define the conviction/confluence criteria that justify dialing per-trade risk toward 3% ("biases aligning").
5. Confirm the exact position-size formula and per-instrument value tables (FX pip value, futures point/tick value, options) and the rounding-down policy.
6. Is the 1-3% band a hard reject, or should the engine auto-resize to the nearest bound?
