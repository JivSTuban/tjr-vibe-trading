1. **Swing-point definition:** How does TJR mechanically identify a swing high / swing low (fractal N-bar, prior candle, close vs wick)? Needed before HH/HL/LH/LL trend classification can be coded. Is this defined in a later lesson?
2. **Break-of-structure threshold:** What exactly breaks a trend — a wick beyond the last higher-low, or a body close? Which swing is "protected" (the most recent HL, or a major/origin HL)? (Deferred to Lesson 06 — confirm there.)
3. **Trend confirmation count:** Does one HH+HL confirm an uptrend, or does TJR require 2+ consecutive higher swings before calling it a trend?
4. **Canonical timeframe stack:** Is there a fixed HTF→LTF mapping for intraday trades (e.g. 4H+daily bias → 30m sweep → 5m BOS), or is it discretionary per pair/holding period?
5. **HTF conflict resolution:** If daily and 4H disagree (e.g. daily down, 4H up), which wins? How many HTFs must align before an LTF entry is permitted?
6. **Impulse>retracement heuristic:** Is "moves with trend are always bigger than moves against" ever used as an actual filter/measurement, or is it purely descriptive?
7. **Consolidation → trend trigger:** What defines a range boundary break that starts a trend (close beyond, penetration amount)? Is this the same threshold as BOS?
8. **Retracement vs reversal:** The lesson says never fade a retracement, but the strategy enters at trend extremes after a sweep. Confirm the line between an acceptable post-sweep reversal entry and a forbidden counter-trend retracement trade.
