# Ambiguities — Lesson 15 (Identifying Problems)

This is a psychology/discipline lesson. Most "rules" are qualitative and lack the
concrete thresholds a risk engine needs. Items below must be resolved by a human
(Jiv) before any rule is coded. Default confidence: low unless noted.

## 1. Max trades per day — is "one" a rule or an example? (confidence: low)
- [06:04–06:09] "I take five trades a day and I say I should only take one."
- The "one" is embedded in TJR's *self-diagnostic* narration, not stated as a
  universal system limit. It could be his personal target or a rhetorical device.
- **Needs:** Jiv to decide the enforced daily trade cap (1? per-session? per-setup?).

## 2. "Stop after a loss" vs "cooldown" — no explicit number (confidence: low)
- [03:54–03:59] revenge trade / "second trade of the day after you lost your first"
  is framed as doomed.
- TJR never states an explicit daily-loss stop count or cooldown duration.
- **Needs:** hard stop after N losses? cooldown minutes? Jiv to define.

## 3. "Reduce risk / go to demo" trigger is undefined (confidence: low)
- [04:54–05:07, 09:05] solutions to fear are "risk less" or "go to demo."
- No target risk %, no size-reduction amount, and no objective definition of what
  programmatically counts as "fear-driven" behavior (early exits, cut before
  SL/TP). "Getting two in my head" [03:55] = ASR for "getting into my head"
  (self-doubt/overthinking) — corrected mentally, non-obvious so noted here.
- **Needs:** measurable trigger + concrete de-risk action.

## 4. Risk % numbers are illustrative, not prescribed here (confidence: low)
- [02:19–02:26] "you risk like 10% of a trade instead of one percent." This appears
  as an *example* of over-leverage, not a stated rule of this lesson. The canonical
  1% figure may be defined authoritatively in another lesson.
- **Needs:** confirm the canonical per-trade risk % from a risk-specific lesson.

## 5. Quote attribution uncertain (confidence: low)
- [10:35] The "boss won't fire you but you must never repeat the mistake" quote is
  attributed by TJR to "Alex Hormosi" (ASR for Alex Hormozi) with explicit
  uncertainty ("I don't know if Alex Hormozi said it"). Not load-bearing for rules;

## 6. Emotion catalog completeness (confidence: medium)
- Emotions named across the lesson: greed, revenge, FOMO, overconfidence, fear /

  [03:31, 05:04, 08:13–08:22]. Whether this is a closed taxonomy or open-ended is
