# Boot Camp Day 50: Weekly Analysis — Lesson Summary

Primary topic: process (weekly top-down bias + news-schedule planning)

## What TJR teaches

**Format shift.** From here the boot camp becomes live trading / daily-bias / backtesting videos on weekdays, psychology on weekends, and one weekly-analysis day [01:20]. "This is the regular stuff I do… everything I use personally to be profitable" [09:56].

### Step 1 — Plan the week's news schedule first
Before bias, TJR maps the economic calendar to decide **which days he'll trade and which he must wait for news on** [02:07]:
- **Monday:** Empire Manufacturing Index ~1h before open — clean; see how it affects the market, then trade [02:18].
- **Tue:** core retail sales / retail sales / industrial production, all pre-market (~1h and 15m before open) — minor; wait for open then trade [02:32].
- **Wed:** **CPI** — will avoid GBP/USD and GBP/JPY that day; USD/SPX pairs still clean. Building permits 1h before open — no big deal [02:51].
- **Thu:** unemployment claims (can heavily move the market or not at all), Philly Fed (minor, same time), existing home sales — be very selective; sometimes pre-market moves so much there's no reason to trade after [03:11].
- **Fri:** clean week besides Thursday [03:54].

Takeaway: **avoid the currency of a high-impact release** (CPI → avoid GBP pairs) and treat pre-market releases as "wait and see."

### Step 2 — Weekly bias, pair by pair (top-down, draw on liquidity)
- **SPX:** broke monthly structure up → next draws on liquidity are the highs above; no building blocks in between, so price should continue up to those highs [04:07]. "That's what trend is saying… I can't give a f*** what fundamentals say — this is what price has given us" [04:27]. A bearish daily candle suggests a daily retrace into a high-confluence zone: **FVG + equilibrium + 4H order block all lined up** [05:13]; got a 4H BOS down, so price may draw into that zone early in the week, then rally to the liquidity draws [05:33].
- **Gold:** weekly downtrend (far past equilibrium) but **daily broke structure up** → look for daily to retrace into a 4H imbalance/order block then a slight move up to catch on a trading day [06:01]. Monthly is an uptrend; if monthly closes with continuation he'll flip bias; a weekly BOS up would target all-time highs [06:48].
- **GBPUSD:** weekly + daily bullish; an order block already tested by a wick; equilibrium in line with an FVG and liquidity → wants price to draw back down into that area during the week. Tentative on GBP pairs due to CPI [07:41].
- **GBPJPY:** weekly still retracing; daily bearish while weekly bullish; popped up into equilibrium and wicked out → looking for a **4H BOS**, then "find little shorts all the way down into weekly equilibrium," with shorter-term take-profits off liquidity/building blocks [08:49].

## Codex interpretation (explicitly inferred — NOT verbatim rules)

- **Weekly routine = two ordered passes:** (1) build a per-day news/trade-eligibility calendar (blackout the affected currency on high-impact days), then (2) run top-down weekly→daily→4H bias per instrument, marking draws on liquidity and high-confluence retracement zones.
- **Draw-on-liquidity targeting:** the weekly TP objective is the next pool of liquidity (prior highs/lows / all-time highs) when no building blocks sit between price and that pool.
- **Price over fundamentals:** bias follows price/structure, not macro narrative — a machine input should never override a structural bias with a fundamental view.
- **News-currency avoidance:** map each release to its currency and blacklist trades in that currency's pairs for the day (CPI→GBP here). Specific window/impact tiers not numerically defined in this lesson.
