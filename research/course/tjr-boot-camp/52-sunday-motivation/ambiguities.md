# Ambiguities — Lesson 52 (Sunday Motivation)

- **Daily-loss stop scope** [08:53] — "I'm done for today" clearly means stop after a loss, but whether the boundary is the trading session or the full calendar day, and whether it's one loss or N losses, is not specified. Needs a human threshold.
- **No numeric limits** — no dollar daily-loss cap, no max-trades-per-session count, no min-sample-size for "judge over a sample."
- **Evaluation windows** [01:11] — "yearly perspective" vs weekly/monthly is qualitative; the metrics layer needs concrete rolling-window lengths chosen by the human.
- **Over-trading-after-win** [01:02] — described behaviorally, not as a hard cap; overlaps the "one trade a day" cadence from lesson 17.
- The bulk of the lesson (meditation, self-worth, loneliness, confidence) is human-development content with no machine-executable rule — captured only as behavioral context.
